Better Walking Cane

A smart cane for safer and more connected mobility to bridge the gap between innovation and accessibility.

Traditional step counters integrated in smartwatches and fitness trackers fail when it comes to atypical or assisted walking patterns. Our smart quadripod cane integrates sensors and movement recognition algorithms capable of identifying real steps while filtering out static supports and noise.

Designed for elderly people, individuals with motor disabilities, or those in rehabilitation, this device enables precise monitoring of physical activity, promoting health, awareness, and autonomy.

What Makes It Special

Smart Recognition

Advanced algorithms distinguish real steps from simple supports, providing accurate activity monitoring for assisted walking.

Universal Compatibility

3d printed design that can be adapted for any standard quadripod cane, making it accessible to existing users.

Open Source

Transparent development focused on accessibility and continuous improvement. Plug and play thanks to Arduino Modulino®

Project Status

A Work in Progress

The project is in the prototyping phase. Initial tests with accelerometers and step classification models are underway. Next steps include validation with different users, time series analysis, integration with a companion app, and system battery optimization.
